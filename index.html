Meet Node.

Node is the device. Inside it lives an AI that knows your work, your voice and your ideas, and keeps them to itself.

It's yours

It runs on a device in your home or office. Your conversations, files and memories stay there.

It never runs out

No tokens, no credits, no meter. Use it all day. It doesn't charge you by the word.

It meets you halfway

It speaks up when it has something worth saying, and makes images, video and 3D with you, not just for you.

Talk

Speak to it like a person. It answers out loud, follows the thread, and picks up where you left off yesterday.

Remember

It remembers what matters to you, and you can see and edit everything it knows.

Create

Images, video and 3D from a conversation. You stay in the director's chair.

Work

Hand off the busywork. A small team of AI helpers runs it in the background and reports back.
